ISTANBUL • CITY GUIDE

Your first 72 hours
in Istanbul

Istanbul is too big to “finish” in a weekend. That's exactly why your first three days should be about rhythm: one historic morning, one ferry crossing, one long meal and plenty of time to wander.

Day 1 — Start in the old city

Begin around Sultanahmet before the crowds build. Give yourself time for the Hagia Sophia area, the Blue Mosque and the surrounding streets. Then walk downhill toward Eminönü and the water.

Twoconts tip
Don't plan every hour. Istanbul rewards you when you leave gaps in your itinerary.

Day 2 — Cross the Bosphorus

Take a public ferry and watch the city change as Europe becomes Asia. Spend the afternoon around Kadıköy and Moda, where cafés, markets and residential streets give you a different view of Istanbul.

Day 3 — Follow your appetite

Make food the itinerary. Start with a bakery breakfast, stop for Turkish coffee, share a few meze dishes at lunch and finish with something grilled. The best meal is often the one you didn't schedule.

The route at a glance

  • Morning: historic peninsula
  • Afternoon: Eminönü → ferry → Kadıköy
  • Evening: neighbourhood dinner
  • Next day: markets, cafés and the Bosphorus
