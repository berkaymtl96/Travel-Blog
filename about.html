ABOUT TWOCONTS

Turkey is not
one story.

Twoconts is an independent travel project about Turkey — a place that refuses to fit neatly into one category.

We started with Istanbul because there is nowhere quite like it: a city connected by water, divided by continents and shaped by thousands of years of movement.

The goal is simple: make travelling in Turkey easier, more interesting and more personal. Expect practical itineraries alongside neighbourhood guides, food recommendations and stories worth saving for later.

“The best way to understand Turkey is to keep moving through it.”
